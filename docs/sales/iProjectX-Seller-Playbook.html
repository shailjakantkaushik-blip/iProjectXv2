How to run the best meeting in the building

This pack is a conversation, not a feature dump. You are selling the next decision, on time, with evidence. If you hear yourself listing modules, stop and go back to the four verbs: See. Explain. Govern. Trust.

Open in 30 seconds

“Most portfolios I sit with still rebuild the board pack on Sunday. RAG is typed. Money cannot explain itself. Gates live in email. iProjectX is the intelligence layer over delivery — calculated health, a pulse of what changed this week, and a gate you can defend. Can I show you that on a live portfolio, then we decide if an Expression of Interest is worth your time?”

Do

  • Ask who rebuilds the pack, who paints RAG, who gets blamed for a late overrun.
  • Demo their pain path: Pulse → one red project → Explain This → one gate.
  • Stay on their scope. Restricted users must see only their programs. That is a feature.
  • Leave the one-pager and the security sheet. Capture EOI the same day.

Never

  • Claim SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification. Say readiness and show controls.
  • Invent customer logos, quotes, or ROI percentages.
  • Turn AI on as a party trick. Lead with in-house AI; mention Open AI only if they ask.
  • Compete as “another register.” If they want a spreadsheet with a login, walk away politely.

Questions that make the pitch land

PMO / Head of delivery

  • How many hours does the last board pack actually take?
  • Who is allowed to change a RAG, and can anyone see that they did?
  • When a gate is “approved,” where does the evidence live?
  • Do Agile and Waterfall report to the same person on the same slide?

Executive / sponsor

  • What do you wish you had known two weeks earlier on the last red program?
  • If I filter to your programs only, what should disappear?
  • What decision keeps slipping because the room argues the data?

CFO / finance partner

  • Where does FAC get challenged — and can anyone show the monthly build-up?
  • Do year allocations ever exceed the lifetime envelope on paper?
  • Are benefits tracked after go-live, or only promised in the case?

CISO / risk

  • Is MFA actually mandatory today, or “strongly recommended”?
  • Can a PM see another division’s RAID?
  • Would you allow a third-party model to read portfolio text?
  • Does any register need to stay on your database?

Live demo path — 12 minutes, no wandering

  1. Home — scoped snapshot. “This is your portfolio, not the whole company.”
  2. Portfolio Pulse / Cockpit — what moved this week. Click one attention card.
  3. Red or Amber project — Health Engine dimensions. Point at the band. Mention the M marker if an override exists.
  4. Explain This on a money KPI. FAC vs envelope. Monthly plan / actual / forecast.
  5. RAID tab — open a risk, show forum + gate. Record a stage-gate decision if they are warm.
  6. Trust, 60 seconds — MFA, Project Access, in-house AI. Hand the security sheet. Stop talking.

Objections

Answer the fear. Do not argue the feature.

“We already have Jira / Project / ServiceNow / Power BI.”

Those are delivery, ticketing, or charts. They do not calculate portfolio health, govern a method-aware gate, and isolate tenants on one spine. Jira can land in Demand. BI can follow. iProjectX is the decision layer.

“Another platform will not land here.”

Start with one Strategic Alignment or one program. Excel import on project code. White-label so the board sees their mark. Project Access so nobody sees a neighbour’s RAID.

“AI will leak the portfolio.”

In-house AI is the default and stays in the org session. An approved Open AI model is off unless they request it. That is a control, not a slogan.

“Our data cannot leave the building.”

Optional BYOD: tenant registers on their PostgREST-compatible database. Auth stays with us. Walk the CISO through the security sheet.

“Are you SOC 2?”

We design for SOC 2 and ISO 27001 readiness. We will not say certified. We will show MFA, RLS, audit, CSP, evidence packs, and the policy set. Offer a control walkthrough.

“PMs will hate another RAG.”

They already have one — they type it. We calculate it, show drivers, and allow a named override so a PM can disagree in the open instead of in the cell.

Leave with a next step, not a compliment

“You have seen health that can defend itself, a pulse of this week, and a gate with evidence. The useful next step is an Expression of Interest so we can scope a working instance on your programs — not a longer slide tour. Who else has to sit in that room: finance, security, or the PMO lead?”

Qualify before you discount

  • A named pack-builder who feels the Sunday pain.
  • A scope (program / SA) they will actually load.
  • A security or residency constraint you can answer with BYOD / MFA / AI.
  • A date for EOI — not “send me something.”
